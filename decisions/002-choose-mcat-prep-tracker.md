# Decision 002: Team sandbox project is the MCAT Prep Tracker

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Status:** Active

## Context

Sprint 1 goal: pick one business idea for the sandbox program so the team can start
interviewing customers and building. We listed three candidates and took all three to the
local Sandbox director. His advice was to start with the idea whose target customers we can
reach most easily.

## Options considered

1. **Medical school test prep (MCAT).** Pros: a real market that has proven it will pay;
   pre-med students are easy for us to reach at BYU. Cons: very crowded (UWorld, AMBOSS,
   Anki, Blueprint, Kaplan), and we haven't found a clear unfair advantage yet.
2. **Daycare "CRM"** (staff log meals and activities, parents view them). Pros: simple,
   well-understood workflow. Cons: 15+ funded competitors already do this, and the total
   market is only about $218M globally. As described, it gives us no real way in.
3. **AI-personalized career/skills coaching for students.** Pros: a real category (BetterUp,
   CoachHub, Torch), and those players sell to companies, not students, so there is a real
   gap at BYU MSB specifically. On paper this was the strongest candidate. Cons: we know
   less about this market, and it is less clear who would pay.

## Decision

MCAT test prep. Deciding reason: I researched this market and its competitors for a strategy
class project last semester, so we start with more market knowledge here than for either
other idea. It also fits the director's advice, since pre-med students are the customers
we can reach most easily.

Other advice from the director (met twice): as students we can iterate faster and respond to
customer requests more flexibly than bigger organizations, and a prototype is enough to show
customers. We don't need a finished product.

## What would change our mind

We revisit this if interviews show one of these:
- students won't pay more than a cheap per-subject subscription (~$4-14/month, per
  [interview 001](../discovery/interviews/001-premed-student.md)), or
- we still can't name an advantage over the incumbents after 5+ interviews.

In that case the fallback is idea 3, career/skills coaching for students.
