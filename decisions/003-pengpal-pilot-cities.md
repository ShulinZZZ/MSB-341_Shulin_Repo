# Decision 003: PengPal pilot cities are Chongqing + Shanghai

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Active

## Context

The PengPal pitch deck contradicted itself: slide 4 said the pilot runs in Chongqing +
Shanghai, but its speaker notes said Chongqing + Chengdu. The prototype
(`specs/004-pengpal-trip-prototype.md`) needed one pair, and the deck needs one pair before
the Top 20 pitch.

## Options considered

1. **Chongqing + Shanghai**: Shanghai is the biggest entry point for foreign visitors, so it
   has the most first-timers to recruit and the strongest returnee host pool. It's also the
   easiest Chinese city to get around alone (more English, international cards more widely
   accepted), so travelers may need us less there. The two cities are far apart (about 1,400
   km), so hand-matching both means two separate host networks.
2. **Chongqing + Chengdu**: Two neighboring Sichuan cities about 1 to 1.5 hours apart by
   high-speed rail, so one small team can recruit and run trips in both. Food is the main draw
   in both. Fewer foreign arrivals than Shanghai.

## Decision

Chongqing + Shanghai, matching the slide as written. The deciding reason: Shanghai has the most
first-time foreign arrivals, and the pilot first has to prove that people will book at all.

## What would change our mind

- Shanghai travelers in early conversations say they don't need help getting around ("I was
  fine on my own") at a noticeably higher rate than Chongqing travelers.
- We can't recruit enough verified hosts in both cities with a team our size, and Chengdu
  hosts are easier to reach through the same networks as Chongqing.

Follow-up: update slide 4's speaker notes to say Chongqing + Shanghai.
