## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I usually watch MCAT review videos and then do practice questions to see whether I understand the material.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

Remembering everything is difficult, especially when different subjects overlap.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I focus on topics I get wrong during practice questions.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use Khan Academy, Anki, and MCAT prep books.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I try to catch up by watching review videos at a faster speed, but I don't always remember the material afterward.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I really like the visual design, especially the mastery map and knowledge tree.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was simple and didn't take much time.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

I liked the explanations, but I think it should include more questions.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

The colors make it easy to see which subjects I'm weak in.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

It's useful because it connects my weak subjects to a weekly study schedule.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

The tree shows how strong my understanding is and how concepts fade when I don't review them.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The knowledge tree because I'm a visual learner and I like seeing my progress.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I wasn't sure whether marking something as reviewed meant I had mastered it.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add short practice quizzes when you click on a concept in the knowledge tree.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, because seeing my knowledge visually would motivate me to keep reviewing.
