## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I've been studying for several months. I mainly focus on practice questions and full-length exams because I've already reviewed most of the content.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

The hardest part is improving my practice test scores. Sometimes I study a lot but don't see much improvement.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I look at the questions I missed on my practice exams and focus on those subjects during the following week.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use UWorld, AAMC practice materials, and Anki.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I usually prioritize my weakest subjects and spend less time on topics I already understand.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I like the idea of focusing on weaknesses instead of reviewing everything equally.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was straightforward. I liked being able to enter my latest practice score.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

Eight questions aren't enough to accurately determine someone's strengths and weaknesses, especially if they've already been studying for a while.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It gives me a general overview of which areas need improvement.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

It could be useful, but I would want the plan to change based on my actual practice exam results.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It seems to represent how well I've retained different concepts.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The mastery map because it helps me identify what I should focus on.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I didn't understand how the app calculated my projected MCAT score.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add the ability to enter practice exam scores and use those results to update the study plan.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Maybe. I would use it if it provided more accurate recommendations than the study schedule I already have.
