## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I haven't really started studying for the MCAT yet. Right now I'm mostly focusing on my introductory science classes and learning about what the exam covers.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

I think the hardest part is not knowing where to start. There are so many subjects, and I'm not sure when I should begin preparing.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I don't have a weekly MCAT schedule. I mostly review whatever we're learning in class.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use my class notes, Khan Academy, and sometimes YouTube videos.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

Since I don't have a formal study plan, I usually just study when I have extra time.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

My first impression is that it's pretty simple and easy to understand. I like that it gives you a plan instead of making you figure everything out yourself.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was easy, but I wasn't sure what target score I should choose because I haven't taken a practice test before.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

I liked the explanations because there were some questions about subjects I haven't learned yet.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It shows me what subjects I already know and what I still need to learn.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

I think it's really helpful for beginners because I wouldn't know how to make an MCAT study plan myself.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It looks like a tree showing different things I need to learn for the MCAT.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The study plan because it tells me where to start.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

Some of the diagnostic questions were difficult because I haven't taken those classes yet.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add an option for students who are just starting pre-med and aren't ready to take the MCAT.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, I would probably use it later when I start preparing more seriously.

## Score-visibility question

**Q16. Can you tell whether your studying is moving your practice scores?**

No, because I haven't started taking scored MCAT practice tests yet.
