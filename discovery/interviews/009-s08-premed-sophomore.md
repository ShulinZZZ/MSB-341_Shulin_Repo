## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I started studying a little while ago, but I haven't been very consistent. I mostly use Anki and watch videos.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

Staying motivated is the hardest part. Sometimes I feel overwhelmed by how much I need to learn.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I usually study whatever topic I feel like working on that day.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use Anki, YouTube, and some online MCAT study guides.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I usually stop studying for a few days and then try to start again.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

It looks motivating because it breaks everything into smaller goals.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was easy, and I liked seeing the countdown to my test date.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

I liked the short quiz because it didn't feel overwhelming.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It shows me what I should focus on instead of trying to study everything.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

The weekly study plan is helpful because smaller goals feel easier to manage.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

I think the tree shows how my knowledge grows when I study and fades when I don't review.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The weekly study cards because they make studying feel more manageable.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

Seeing so many weak areas at once made me feel a little discouraged.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add daily goals and a progress streak to help students stay motivated.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, because I need something that helps me stay consistent.
