## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I'm not preparing for the MCAT yet. Right now I'm trying to do well in my science classes and understand what medical school requires.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

I think the most difficult part is knowing what I should learn now to prepare for the exam later.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I don't have an MCAT study schedule yet. I mainly review my class material.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use textbooks, class notes, and YouTube.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I usually focus on my assignments first and study extra material when I have free time.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I think the app looks professional and easy to navigate.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was easy, but I wasn't sure what score I should aim for.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

Some questions were too advanced for me, but I liked reading the explanations.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It shows which subjects I haven't learned yet.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

I think the plan would be useful later, but right now I need something that helps me prepare gradually.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It represents different MCAT concepts and how well I understand them.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The mastery map because it gives me an overview of what I need to learn.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I didn't understand why the app rated me as weak in subjects I haven't studied yet.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add an option to indicate which prerequisite classes I've already completed.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

I would use it later, especially when I begin studying seriously for the MCAT.

## Pain point and requested change

- **Confusing or difficult (Q13):** "I didn't understand why the app rated me as weak in subjects I haven't studied yet."
- **Would change (Q14):** "I would add an option to indicate which prerequisite classes I've already completed."
- **Would use it (Q15):** "I would use it later, especially when I begin studying seriously for the MCAT."

## Score-visibility question

**Q16. Can you tell whether your studying is moving your practice scores?**

No. I haven't taken practice tests, so I don't have any scores to track.
