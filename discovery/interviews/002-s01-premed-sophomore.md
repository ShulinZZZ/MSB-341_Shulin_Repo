## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I'm using some online resources I bought and preparing for the exam on my own during my free time outside of school. I haven't started doing full-length practice exams yet.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

It's challenging to remember all the different subjects I've studied. I have to frequently remind myself to review them, especially biology and chemistry concepts.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I usually follow my prep books and review topics related to my current classes. I don't really have a strict weekly schedule.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use Kaplan books, Anki flashcards, and YouTube videos when I don't understand something.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I usually try to catch up over the weekend, but sometimes I just move on because I don't have enough time.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I like how organized everything looks. It makes studying for the MCAT seem less overwhelming.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was pretty easy. I liked that I could choose my target score and test date without going through too many steps.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

I liked getting explanations after each question. I think it would be better with more questions so the results are more accurate.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It shows me which subjects I'm doing well in and which ones I need to spend more time reviewing.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

It's helpful because I don't always know what I should study next.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

I think it shows how well I remember different concepts and how my knowledge changes over time.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The knowledge tree because I like being able to see what I might be forgetting.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I wasn't sure what all the colors on the knowledge tree meant at first.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add reminders for when I need to review certain concepts.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, because I need help organizing my studying and remembering what I've already learned.
