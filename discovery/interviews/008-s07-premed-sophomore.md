## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I've started doing some light MCAT preparation, mostly using free resources when I have time.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

Finding good study materials without spending too much money.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I follow free study guides I find online and review topics from my science classes.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use Khan Academy, YouTube, and free practice questions.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I usually continue where I left off instead of trying to catch up with everything.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I like that it brings the study plan and progress information together.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was really easy to enter my goals.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

I liked getting immediate feedback after each question.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It shows me which subjects I should review first.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

It's useful because I don't have to spend time searching for a study schedule online.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It shows how recently I've reviewed different topics.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The personalized study plan because it recommends what I should study each week.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I wasn't sure whether the videos and other resources would cost money.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add more free resources and make it clear which materials are free.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, especially if the main features were free to use.
