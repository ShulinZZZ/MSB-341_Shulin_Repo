## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I'm taking several science classes right now, so I usually study for the MCAT at night or during weekends.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

Time management is definitely the hardest part. It's difficult to balance MCAT preparation with schoolwork.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I plan my studying around my assignments and exams. Some weeks I can study a lot, and other weeks I barely have time.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use Kaplan books, YouTube, and Google Calendar to organize my schedule.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I usually move unfinished topics to the next week, but then everything starts piling up.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I like the clean design and how it organizes everything into weekly tasks.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was easy, but I wish it asked how many hours I could study each week.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

I liked that the diagnostic was short because I don't always have time for long assessments.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It helps me understand which topics need more attention.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

I think the study plan is useful, but it needs to work around my class schedule.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It shows which concepts I remember well and which ones I might be forgetting.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The weekly study plan because scheduling is my biggest problem.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I couldn't find a way to change the plan if I missed a week.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add automatic rescheduling when students fall behind.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, if it could adjust to my schedule instead of expecting me to follow the same plan every week.
