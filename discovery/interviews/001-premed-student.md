# Interview: P1, pre-med student, BYU, self-studying for the MCAT (~1/3 through prep)

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Interviewers:** Me and a teammate (from the meeting recap)
**How I found them:** Peer in the Sandbox program
**Persona:** [discovery/personas.md](../personas.md) (not yet written, needs more interviews)

## What I asked / what happened

Asked about how P1 is preparing for the MCAT, what they pay for, and what is hardest. P1
started studying July 1 and plans to test in March, possibly pushing to April or May
depending on workload. P1 studies independently: no live classes, no paid tutoring
package. They looked at Princeton Review and Khan Academy before settling on a smaller
subscription course. P1 called the MCAT "make or break": a near-perfect score can outweigh
average undergrad grades.

At the end we showed the prototype. P1 agreed to try it and send feedback.

## What they actually do today

- **Main resource:** a small online course run by a family friend who used to work at
  Princeton Review and is now a med school admit who scores near-perfect every year. It is
  set up like a Canvas module: practice questions → videos → note packets → **teach the
  material back** (even to an object) → new practice questions to measure improvement.
- **Pricing they chose:** monthly, per subject: $4-9 per topic, about $14/month combined.
  P1 compared this with $2,000-4,000 upfront for Princeton Review-style packages.
- **Most helpful thing overall:** free one-on-one help from that same family friend.
- **Other tools:** Anki flashcards. A peer uses AI (Claude) to generate personalized study
  material and resurface missed questions more often.

## Strongest signal

**Price sensitivity.** P1 said pre-meds are very price-sensitive and wait for sales
(e.g. Labor Day discounts) before buying materials. They picked a $9-14/month per-subject
course over a $2,000-4,000 package. Close second: **CARS is the hardest section**, because
it is reading comprehension and "requires different preparation" from the memorization-heavy
sections.
