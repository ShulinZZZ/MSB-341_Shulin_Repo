## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I haven't started preparing for the MCAT yet. I'm still learning about the requirements for medical school.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

The biggest challenge is understanding how all the science classes connect to the MCAT.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I don't choose MCAT topics yet. I mainly focus on understanding the subjects I'm taking this semester.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use class textbooks, lecture slides, and free online videos.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

If I fall behind in school, I focus on catching up with my classes before doing any extra MCAT preparation.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

It looks interesting, and I like that it shows the different subjects on one screen.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was easy, but I don't know my test date or target score yet.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

Some questions were unfamiliar because I haven't taken organic chemistry or physics.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It shows what I know now, but I don't think it's completely accurate because I haven't learned everything yet.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

I think it would be more useful when I'm closer to taking the MCAT.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It looks like a map of all the concepts I need to learn.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The mastery map because it helps me understand how much material the MCAT covers.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I felt a little overwhelmed seeing so many weak subjects.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add an option that creates a long-term preparation plan based on the classes students have completed.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

I would probably use it in my sophomore or junior year when I start studying more seriously.

## Pain point and requested change

- **Confusing or difficult (Q13):** "I felt a little overwhelmed seeing so many weak subjects."
- **Would change (Q14):** "I would add an option that creates a long-term preparation plan based on the classes students have completed."
- **Would use it (Q15):** "I would probably use it in my sophomore or junior year when I start studying more seriously."

## Score-visibility question

**Q16. Can you tell whether your studying is moving your practice scores?**

No. I haven't taken any MCAT practice exams, so I don't have a score to compare.
