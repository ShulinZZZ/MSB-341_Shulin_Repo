## Part A: MCAT preparation experience

**Q1. Can you tell me about your current MCAT preparation process?**

I created my own study schedule using Google Sheets. I track the topics I study and plan review sessions throughout the semester.

**Q2. What is the most challenging part of studying for the MCAT?**

Keeping track of everything and knowing when to review older topics.

**Q3. How do you decide which topics to study each week?**

I use a spreadsheet to assign topics to each week and adjust it depending on my progress.

**Q4. What tools or resources do you currently use to prepare?**

I use Google Sheets, Anki, Kaplan books, and AAMC materials.

**Q5. What happens when you fall behind on your study schedule?**

I manually move unfinished tasks to another day, but reorganizing everything takes time.

## Part B: Gapmap prototype feedback

**Q6. What is your first impression of Gapmap?**

I like that Gapmap combines planning and progress tracking into one interface.

**Q7. How easy or difficult was it to set your MCAT goals?**

It was simple, but I wanted more options to customize my study schedule.

**Q8. What do you think about the diagnostic assessment?**

The diagnostic was a good starting point, but I would prefer a longer assessment.

**Q9. What does the mastery map communicate to you?**

It gives me a quick summary of my strengths and weaknesses.

**Q10. How useful is the personalized study plan?**

It's useful, but I would want to edit the weekly plan myself.

**Q11. What do you think the knowledge tree represents?**

It represents how strong my understanding is across different MCAT concepts.

**Q12. Which feature did you find most useful, and why?**

The knowledge tree because it could help me decide when to review topics.

**Q13. What was confusing, frustrating, or difficult to use?**

I couldn't mark study tasks as completed or change the weekly schedule.

**Q14. What is one thing you would change about Gapmap?**

I would add editable study plans, completion checkboxes, and calendar integration.

**Q15. Would you use Gapmap while preparing for the MCAT? Why or why not?**

Yes, if it could replace my spreadsheet and automatically track my progress.
