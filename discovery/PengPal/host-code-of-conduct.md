# English

## 1. What you are

You're a **language-exchange friend**. You meet a first-time visitor to practice English and make
a friend from abroad, and you help them feel at home in daily life: ordering food, taking the
metro, a neighborhood you like.

You are **not a tour guide**, and PengPal doesn't pay you for meetups. That's the reason for the
rules in section 2. In China, paid guiding needs a guide certificate and an agency. If you already
hold a guide certificate, you still meet travelers as a friend through PengPal, never as their
guide.

## 2. Meetups

**Do:**
- Meet once per traveler group in the pilot, **in daytime, in a public place** (a café,
  restaurant, metro station or park).
- Do everyday things together: eat, ride the metro, walk a neighborhood, show them how to scan a
  QR menu or pay with Alipay.
- Tell PengPal the plan before you go: the time, the place and who you're meeting.
- Send PengPal a message when you meet and when you part.

**Don't:**
- Pick travelers up at the airport or station, or drive them anywhere.
- Host them at your home, or visit their hotel room.
- Give paid commentary at sights, or plan or sell a sightseeing route.
- Book tickets, hotels or transport for them.
- Call yourself a guide (导游), or use the words 陪游, 带团 or 包车.

## 3. Money

- **Never take money from a traveler,** whether cash, WeChat or Alipay transfers, or red packets
  (红包).
- If you eat or go somewhere together, the traveler pays for your share **at the place**, the
  same way friends treat each other. Nothing else is paid to you.
- **Paid pre-trip calls are the one exception.** They're 30-minute video calls from home before
  the traveler arrives, and PengPal pays you **$20 per call**, as a lump sum after the call, with
  a written note. That's taxable income in China, and you declare it yourself in your annual tax
  return as labor income (劳务报酬).
- Rewards for meetups: English practice, friends abroad, and a PengPal reference letter or
  certificate after your first 3 meetups.

## 4. Safety

These rules protect you as much as the traveler.
- **PengPal checks every traveler too.** You'll see their first name, home country and trip
  dates, and you meet them on a video intro before any meetup.
- **You can say no to any traveler, before or after the intro, without giving a reason.** You can
  also ask to meet only women travelers.
- **Either of you can end a meetup at any time.** Leave and message PengPal. You don't need to
  explain.
- If anything feels wrong, go to a busy place and message PengPal. In an emergency, call **110**
  (police) or **120** (ambulance) first.
- Travelers must carry travel insurance and sign a waiver before meeting you.

## 5. How matching works

PengPal matches you with travelers based on:
- the kind of traveler they are (family, solo, student and so on)
- shared interests
- languages
- where you've lived and where they're from

You both see each other's profile, then meet on a short video intro. **If it doesn't click, either
of you can pass** without any problem. PengPal will find you a better match.

## 6. Behavior

- Treat travelers with respect, and expect the same from them.
- Avoid military or restricted areas and photos of sensitive sites. Stay out of political
  activities.
- Don't preach religion, and don't join a traveler who does. Foreigners face restrictions on
  religious activity in China, and this protects both of you.
- **Never give or recommend a VPN** to a traveler.
- No drugs, no illegal activity. Drinking is fine in moderation, but don't drink to excess.

## 7. Privacy

- PengPal stores only your first name, WeChat ID, languages and experience. It never stores your
  ID number or ID photos.
- Your information is stored outside China, and you've agreed to that. You can ask PengPal to
  delete it at any time.
- Keep travelers' details private. Don't post photos of them without asking.

## 8. Reporting a problem

Tell PengPal within 24 hours if a traveler made you uncomfortable, broke these rules, or if
anything went wrong. Reports are private, separate from public reviews, and PengPal can remove a
traveler from the service.

## 9. Leaving

You can stop being a host at any time. Tell PengPal and we'll delete your information.

# 中文版

## 1. 你的角色

你是一位**语言交换朋友**。你和第一次来中国的外国游客见面，练习英语、交一个外国朋友，并帮他们适应日常生活：点餐、坐地铁、逛你喜欢的街区。

你**不是导游**，PengPal 也不为见面付你报酬。第 2 条的规则就是为此而设：在中国，有偿带游需要导游证，并由旅行社委派。即使你持有导游证，通过 PengPal 见面时也只是朋友，不是导游。

## 2. 见面

**可以：**
- 试点期间每组游客见面一次，**白天、在公共场所**（咖啡馆、餐厅、地铁站、公园）。
- 一起做日常的事：吃饭、坐地铁、逛街区，教他们扫码点餐、用支付宝付款。
- 出发前把时间、地点和见面对象告诉 PengPal。
- 见到面和分开时各给 PengPal 发一条消息。

**不可以：**
- 去机场或车站接人，或开车载游客。
- 让游客住在你家，或去游客的酒店房间。
- 在景点收费讲解，或规划、出售观光路线。
- 替游客订票、订酒店或订交通。
- 自称导游，或使用“陪游”“带团”“包车”等说法。

## 3. 钱

- **绝不收游客的钱**：现金、微信或支付宝转账、红包都不行。
- 如果一起吃饭或去某个地方，游客**当场**替你付你那一份，就像朋友之间请客一样。除此之外没有任何报酬。
- **唯一的例外是付费行前通话**：游客出发前，你在家里和他们视频 30 分钟，PengPal 在通话后一次性付你**每次 20 美元**，并留有书面记录。这在中国属于应税收入，需要你在年度个税汇算中自行申报为劳务报酬。
- 见面的回报：练习英语、结交外国朋友；完成前 3 次见面后，PengPal 提供推荐信或证书。

## 4. 安全

这些规则保护你，也保护游客。
- **PengPal 也会审核每一位游客**：你会看到对方的名字、国家和行程日期，并且在见面前先视频认识。
- **在视频前后，你都可以拒绝任何游客，不需要理由。** 你也可以要求只和女性游客见面。
- **双方都可以随时结束见面**：离开，然后告诉 PengPal，不需要解释。
- 如果感觉不对，去人多的地方，并给 PengPal 发消息。紧急情况先打 **110**（报警）或 **120**（急救）。
- 游客必须购买旅行保险，并在见面前签署免责声明。

## 5. 怎么匹配

PengPal 根据以下几点为你匹配游客：
- 游客的类型（家庭、独自旅行、学生等）
- 共同兴趣
- 语言
- 你住过的国家和他们来自哪里

你们先互相看资料，再进行一次简短的视频认识。**如果不合适，任何一方都可以放弃**，没有任何问题，PengPal 会帮你找更合适的人。

## 6. 行为

- 尊重游客，也期待游客同样尊重你。
- 避开军事或限制区域，不拍敏感设施，不参与政治活动。
- 不传教，也不陪同传教的游客。外国人在中国从事宗教活动受到限制，这样做是保护你们双方。
- **绝不向游客提供或推荐 VPN。**
- 不碰毒品，不做违法的事。可以适量饮酒，但不要喝多。

## 7. 隐私

- PengPal 只保存你的名字、微信号、语言和经历，绝不保存身份证号或证件照片。
- 你的信息存储在中国境外，你已经同意。你可以随时要求 PengPal 删除。
- 不泄露游客的个人信息；未经同意不发布他们的照片。

## 8. 报告问题

如果游客让你感到不舒服、违反了这些规则，或发生了任何问题，请在 24 小时内告诉 PengPal。报告是私下的，与公开评价分开；PengPal 可以将游客移出服务。

## 9. 退出

你可以随时停止做 PengPal 朋友。告诉我们，我们会删除你的信息。
