## Recruiting notes

**Where asking worked, and where it didn't:** Personal networks worked. All 3 hosts came from close ties: a WeChat friend (Nov 1), a cousin's referral (Nov 5) and a classmate (Nov 7). About 10 people were asked in total and 8 said yes. H3 agreed on Nov 7, a day after the Nov 6 target.

**Candidates who said no, and why:** 2 of about 10 declined; reasons not recorded yet. On decision 005's open question 1 (will hosts join unpaid?): 2 of 3 recruited hosts accept unpaid language exchange, and 1 (H3) prefers some pay. All 3 accept paid $20 pre-trip calls.

**What rewards hosts actually asked for:** English practice (H1), foreign friends (H2), sharing Chinese culture (H3), and a little pay (H3).

**Questions hosts asked that the code of conduct should answer:**

- Safety for young female hosts meeting travelers they don't know (H1)
- How travelers and hosts are matched on personality (H2)
