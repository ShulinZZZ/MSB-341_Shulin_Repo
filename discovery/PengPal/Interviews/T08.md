## Their trip and worries

An entrepreneur who imports consumer products from Asia and is going to Guangzhou and Shenzhen in November
2026 to meet suppliers. This is a work trip, not a holiday, and it isn't their first time in China: they've
been twice before and got by with hotel concierge services and translation apps.

The worry is lost time and costly misunderstandings during supplier meetings, not getting lost as a tourist.

tourism. They also don't pass the screener, so this is a side signal, not the core user.
