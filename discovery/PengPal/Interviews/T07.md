## Their trip and worries

An experienced solo traveler (South Korea, Vietnam, Thailand) planning Shanghai, Hangzhou and Chengdu in
May 2027. She plans with travel forums and map apps other than Google Maps.

She likes traveling alone and doesn't want a guide for the whole trip. What she misses is a trusted local
contact when she first arrives somewhere unfamiliar.

## Strongest signal

"I'd probably book someone for my first day, just to feel comfortable navigating the city afterward."

That suggests a distinct product: a first-day orientation for solo travelers. One booking right after
arrival to learn the city, then she goes off on her own. For her, safety is what decides
whether they book: host gender choice, verification, reporting, and live support on the day.
