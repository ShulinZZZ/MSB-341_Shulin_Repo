## Their trip and worries

A university student who has traveled around Europe and will spend a semester in China starting February
2027, based in Beijing and Shanghai. They've joined university exchange groups and read up on student life
in Beijing.

They aren't worried about getting by. They're worried about cost: they want to meet people and explore
without spending much on tourist activities.

## Strongest signal

day. They're very price sensitive. The $24 visit works, but $24 + 15% (about $28) is already near their
$35 walk-away, and they hesitated at the service fee in the flow. They didn't join the waitlist.
