A student who served a Mandarin-speaking mission but has never been to mainland China. She spent a summer
in Taiwan on an exchange program. She's planning a four-city trip next summer with friends.

"I can't speak Chinese well so I am afraid local people can not understand me when I try to order something."

both well below the $80 full day, and she asked for a multi-day discount without being prompted.
