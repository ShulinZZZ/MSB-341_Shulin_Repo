# PengPal competitor positioning

**Date:** 2026-11-23 (Sprint 5)
**Builds on:** the first pass in `mentor-feedback-sprint-4.md` (Mentor 1: "know the existing
alternatives and find your position"). Prices were checked on the web on Nov 23, 2026, and come
from public listings. They change often and aren't quotes. Vendor claims are marked as claims.

## The question

When a first-time traveler worries about getting around China, being understood and paying for
things, what do they use instead of PengPal? And where does PengPal sit among those options, given
that it can't sell paid guiding (decisions 004, 005)?

## Where PengPal sits

Two questions separate the options. **Is it a person or a tool?** And **is it built for the whole
trip, or for one day or one booking?**

| | One day or one booking | The whole trip |
|---|---|---|
| **A person** | Private guides, group tours, local-host listings | **PengPal**, or a friend in China |
| **A tool** | Booking platforms | Free guides, AI travel apps, language-exchange apps |

Every paid option in the "person" row is priced per day and sells guiding. The free options cover
the whole trip but leave the traveler alone with a tool. PengPal is the only option here that's a
person, covers the whole trip, and costs about what one cheap tour does.

## Positioning statement

**For** first-time travelers to Chongqing or Shanghai who worry about getting around and being
understood, **PengPal is** a trip pass with a vetted local friend their age who wants to practice
English, **unlike** a paid guide or a travel app, **because** it's a real person for the whole
trip, for about $49, and hosts who lived in the traveler's country understand both cultures.

## What this means for Sprint 5

- **AI apps are the competitor to beat, not guides.** The 3 travelers who didn't pay chose AI and
  online tips. The pass has to make clear what a person does that an app can't: meet you, vouch
  for a place, talk to a driver or a shop for you.
- **Price looks strong next to guides.** At $49 for the trip, PengPal is a fraction of one guided
  day ($186–$396). The `?pass=` price test (spec 006) can check whether a higher price still sells.
- **The language-exchange model is also the legal position.** Paid local-host marketplaces sell
  what decision 004 rules out. PengPal's unpaid-host model is both its difference and its way of
  staying legal.
