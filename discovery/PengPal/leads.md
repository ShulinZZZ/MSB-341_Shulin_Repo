# PengPal leads: "Join the first trips" sign-ups

**Started:** 2026-11-23
**Source:** the sign-up form on the PengPal page (spec 007). Each submission arrives in Formspree
and by email.
**Sprint 5 milestone (by Sat Nov 28):** 2 or more rows with qualified = yes, knows team = N and
booked = Y.

**Rules:**

- No names, emails, phone numbers or free-text answers in this file. They stay in Formspree and our
  inbox. Each lead gets a code (L01, L02…) and only the fields below.
- **Qualified** comes from the form: yes if the trip month is this month or one of the next 6.
- **Knows team** is the traveler's own answer to "Do you know anyone on the PengPal team?". Also
  mark Y if we find out they're a friend, family member or BYU contact of anyone on the team, or
  were referred by one. Those leads don't count toward the milestone.
- **Channel** is the form's `channel` field (the `?src=` value in the link), or "direct".
- When a lead has a commitment-test session, put its T-note code in the last column.
