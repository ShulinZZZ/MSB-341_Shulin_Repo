# Sprint 3 Plan

**Goal:** Test the PengPal prototype with 10 potential travelers.

**Why this:** To see if travelers would actually pay before building more.

**Done looks like:**
- The PengPal prototype (`product/pengpal/`) is updated so travelers can find a local host,
  schedule an introductory video call, and book a trip, before testing starts.
- 10 potential travelers (people planning a first-time trip to China in the next year) have
  tested the prototype at the PengPal link.
- One anonymized notes file per session in `discovery/` (e.g. `discovery/pengpal/T01`–`T10`).
- Every traveler is asked the same pay question ("Would you pay for this, and how much?") and
  is invited to join a waitlist; answers and waitlist sign-ups are recorded.
- A findings write-up in `discovery/` concludes whether travelers want this enough to pay, at
  what price, and what in the prototype confused them.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3
