# Sprint 1 Plan

**Goal:** I will decide what project my team wants to work on for the sandbox and start interviewing customers and building prototypes.

**Why this:** Because for the sandbox program we need to decide what business idea to do and build solutions/softwares/services for it. So I need to first and quickly decide on what to work on and start reaching out to people.

**Done looks like:** Reach out to at least ten people and set up at least five interviews. List at least three business ideas and talked to at least three professors to get advice. By the end of day 14, my team has decided on and is working on one idea, and has reached out to people related to that idea. Have at least a basic three-page prototype built.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3

*Added at the end of the sprint, after reading the review.*

**Actual difficulty:** 4

**Why it differed:** Building and deploying the prototype went fine. Outreach was harder: it
takes longer to get people to reply and book a time than I planned for.

**Retro:** Partly. We hit 3 of 6 done-criteria: 3 ideas listed, the team chose the MCAT Prep
Tracker ([decision 002](../decisions/002-choose-mcat-prep-tracker.md)), and a three-page
prototype is live. We missed the outreach targets: 6 of 10 people reached, 3 of 5 interviews
set up (1 done, 2 scheduled), and 1 of 3 advisors talked to (1 more meeting scheduled). I put
too much time into building and deploying before the customer conversations were far enough
along, and I didn't log outreach as it happened. I also committed this plan late.
The one interview already challenged our assumptions. Pre-meds are very price-sensitive,
CARS is the section they struggle with most, and the student never mentioned our core
problem, not being able to see whether studying is moving their score.
**Next sprint:** customer conversations come first. Finish the 2 scheduled interviews and the
pre-health advisor meeting, reach 4 more students (P1 offered intros), and ask directly about
the score-visibility problem. Log each contact when I make it, and commit the plan on day one.
