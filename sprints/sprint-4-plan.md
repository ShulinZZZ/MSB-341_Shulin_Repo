# Sprint 4 Plan

**Goal:** Add traveler-type options and the all-in price to the PengPal prototype, design a logo,
recruit real hosts, check the legal concerns on the China side, and test whether travelers will
commit (a real intro call or a deposit); define PengPal's target customers (personas, segments,
early adopters, ideal customer profile, customer journey, channels), and get feedback from mentors.

**Why this:** To see if travelers will actually commit and pay.

**Done looks like:**

*Week 1 (through Sun Nov 1): build and prepare*
- By Fri Oct 30, first: a China legal check in `discovery/PengPal/legal.md`, with sources. It
  covers paid local guides and licensing, paying hosts (cross-border payments, host taxes),
  traveler safety and liability, and data. It ends with what PengPal can and can't do in its
  pilot, including whether it can take deposits.
- Prototype v2 is live, with a spec written first:
  - Onboarding asks what type of traveler you are (family, solo, senior, business, student),
    and the answer changes which hosts show first.
  - Host profiles show that experience.
  - The full price, fee included, appears from the first time a price shows up.
- A new PengPal logo is in the prototype and the README.
- Target customers, part 1, in `discovery/PengPal/market.md`: personas, segments, early adopters
  with their pain points, and an ideal customer profile, built from the T01–T10 data.

*Week 2 (Nov 2–9): test*
- By Fri Nov 6: 3 real hosts recruited in Shanghai, logged without names in
  `discovery/PengPal/hosts.md`.
- 8 traveler sessions on v2, from the waitlist plus new people, with notes in T11–T18.
  - Every traveler is asked for a real commitment: book a real intro call with a host, or put
    down a $10 deposit.
  - If the legal check rules out deposits, or the hosts aren't ready, the commitment is booking
    a specific trip date on the waitlist.
  - The number who commit is recorded.
- Target customers, part 2, in `market.md`: the customer journey from awareness to purchase with
  its key impact points, and the channels to reach the ideal customer.
- Feedback from 2 mentors, written up in notes.
- A findings write-up: how many committed, at what price, and from which segment.

*Moved to Sprint 5:* a bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM and a competitor positioning table.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3
